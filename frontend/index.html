METHODOLOGY

How Skillora Works

Four disciplined steps transforming raw document text into actionable career trajectory intelligence.

01

Upload & Parse

Upload your resume in PDF format. Skillora extracts text across multi-column layouts, tables, and unusual section headings entirely in memory.

02

Contextual Extraction

Our NLP engine identifies hundreds of modern technical competencies, normalizing aliases (e.g., ReactJS = React, ML = Machine Learning).

03

Explainable Match

We classify competencies into Matched, Partially Supported, and Missing, citing exact sentences and lines from both documents.

04

7-Day Action Sprint

Receive a structured daily roadmap, priority ranking, interactive What-If simulator, and alternative career recommendations.

Engineered for Career Precision

Contextual Evidence Citations

Every detected competency is linked to the exact sentence where it was proven in your resume, while missing skills cite the job description requirement lines.
